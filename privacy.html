Drawtrack — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Drawtrack is a personal, single-user web app for tracking my own drawing practice. It is operated for personal use, not as a commercial service.

What data the app handles

  • Practice data (the minutes I log, goals, tags, and saved references) is stored on my own device in the browser and, optionally, synced to a Google Sheet in my own Google account.
  • Instagram & Pinterest content belonging to my own accounts may be displayed inside the app (e.g. my posts and my board images) when I choose to connect those services. This access is read-only.
  • Reference images may be copied into a folder in my own Google Drive so they display reliably.

How third-party access is used

If connected, the Pinterest and Instagram/Meta APIs are used only to read content from my own accounts so it can be shown to me inside the app. The app does not post on my behalf, and it does not access other users' data.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Account access tokens are stored privately in my own Google Apps Script backend and are never exposed in the app.

Data retention & deletion

All data lives in my own device storage and my own Google account, and can be deleted by me at any time (clearing the app's data, deleting the Google Sheet/Drive folder, or disconnecting the linked services).

Contact

This is a personal project. Questions can be directed to the account owner.
